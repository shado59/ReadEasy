ReadEasy - Making Learning Effortless 🚀
Welcome to ReadEasy, a platform built to transform the way students read, learn, and study.

⚠️ The Problem
For many students—especially those with ADHD, Dyslexia, or those learning a new language (ESL)—traditional textbooks are overwhelming. Dense walls of text, complex jargon, and long chapters cause cognitive overload. This makes reading stressful, exhausting, and ultimately ineffective.

💡 The Idea
What if we could turn any complex study material into a highly engaging, interactive, and bite-sized learning experience? We wanted to create a tool that acts as a personal tutor: someone who reads the complicated textbook and explains it back to you in simple terms, step-by-step, while checking if you actually understood it.

🛠️ How We Solved It (The Flow)
ReadEasy takes the stress out of studying through a simple, user-friendly process:

Input Material: The user can paste a long text, upload a PDF, or take a photo of a textbook page.
AI Simplification: Our AI instantly analyzes the material and breaks it down into "slides". The user can choose the reading level: Very Simple, Simple, or Original.
Read & Listen: The student goes through the bite-sized slides. We also included a Text-to-Speech feature so auditory learners can listen while they read.
Knowledge Check: After reading, the system automatically generates an interactive Quiz based on the text.
AI Weak Point Analysis: When the quiz is submitted, the platform calculates a score. If the user makes mistakes, the AI generates a customized "Weak Point Report," explaining exactly where they went wrong and teaching the specific rule or fact they missed.
✨ Key Features
Clean, ADHD-Friendly UI: A modern, distraction-free interface with a calming Dark Mode.
Multimodal Uploads: Support for text, images (JPG/PNG), and PDFs.
Adaptive Difficulty: Change the complexity of the text on the fly.
Interactive Quizzes & AI Feedback: Active recall testing and personalized AI reports.
Accessibility First: Voice reader and visually distinct UI elements to support all learners
